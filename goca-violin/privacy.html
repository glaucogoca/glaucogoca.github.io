Goca Violin — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Goca Violin does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What stays on your device

Your preferences (language, theme, tempo, time signature, drone type) and whether you own the Pro version are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else, and they are erased when you uninstall the app.

Purchases

The Pro version is bought through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; we never see your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether your account owns Pro.

No ads, no tracking

The app has no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking, and it does not need an account.

Children

Because no personal data is collected, the app is safe for students of any age.
